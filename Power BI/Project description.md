# Sales Performance Dashboard

## Project overview
This project focuses on analyzing the sales performance of a retail business using Microsoft Power BI. The analysis transforms raw sales data into an interactive dashboard that provides insights into sales, profit, orders, product categories, and overall business performance.

The goal of the main obejective of this project is to analyze sales,orders,quantity,profits,monitor business KPI to help management understand business performance, identify sales trends, monitor profitability, and make informed business decisions.

## Business problem 
The bussiness tends to solve problems that includes:
1.What is the total revenue?
2.How many orders were taken?
3. how much profit are made in a year?
4.what is the average order value?
5.which sales has the most revenue?
6.which of the sales person sold the most item?
7.what category of the goods are most sold
8.How does the profit margin changes overtime.
9.which state sold the most goods?
10.which product generates the most profit.
11.what are the business opportunities
12.where shoud be the management focus.i.e the business weaknesses.
